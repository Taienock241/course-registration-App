About the Course

Technology Programs focus on the design, development, implementation, and management of modern technological systems. You learn how to build innovative solutions using diverse tools, teamwork, and strong problem-solving skills across various technology domains.

Duration

Four (4) Years

Award

Bachelor of Science

Strength

Practical + Industry Experience

Computer Systems Lab

Hands-on systems architecture, hardware configuration, networking practice.

Study Pods

Quiet learning zones, peer-to-peer study sessions, technology research.

Entry Requirements

  1. Minimum KCSE mean grade of C+
  2. Mathematics C+ and above
  3. Basic computer knowledge
